## Overview

The **Emissions Data Platform** is an open-source initiative designed to track, analyze, and visualize municipal greenhouse gas emissions data to support climate action planning and public policy decisions.

## Key Contributions & Architecture

- **Frontend & Data Visualization**: Developed responsive data exploratory tools and dashboards with **Vue.js** and **GraphQL** queries to display granular emissions trends across sectors.
- **Data Engineering**: Processed and normalized complex multi-year emissions datasets using **Python** and **Pandas**.
- **Automated Testing & CI/CD**: Engineered reliable end-to-end integration test suites with **Playwright**, integrated directly into GitHub Actions workflows to catch regressions before deployments.
- **Agile Collaboration**: Worked with a distributed team of engineers and domain experts through code reviews, sprint planning, and issue tracking.
